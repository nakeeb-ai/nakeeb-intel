## WRITING RULES
1. **Evidence-backed:** Every claim → [source: field]
2. **No hallucination:** Unknown = "Unknown" or "Not documented"
3. **Quantify:** Use numbers from metadata (stars, commits, downloads)
4. **Distinguish:** Verified facts vs. assessments (marked)
5. **Concise:** Target 2000-3000 chars for standard brief
6. **Actionable:** Recommendation must be justified
